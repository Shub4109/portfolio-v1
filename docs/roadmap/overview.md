## Purpose

This document supplements `ROADMAP.md` at the repository root with extended context on each phase — rationale, dependencies, and success criteria per phase.

---

## Phase 1 — Foundation

**Goal:** Ship a production-ready platform with all core pages before any marketing begins.

**Rationale:** Without a live, complete platform, no amount of LinkedIn activity or SEO effort has a destination to convert visitors into leads. Phase 1 is the minimum viable consulting asset.

**Dependencies:** Application scaffold (Sprint 1) must complete before any page work begins.

**Phase 1 Success:** All core pages live, Lighthouse scores meeting targets, contact form working end-to-end, domain and SSL configured.

---

## Phase 2 — Growth

**Goal:** Drive organic traffic and begin building an owned audience.

**Rationale:** A static platform with no content engine will plateau at whatever traffic referrals provide. Blog content and case studies are the primary compounding growth mechanism — every article published increases surface area for organic search discovery.

**Dependencies:** Phase 1 complete; editorial calendar prepared; minimum 4 articles drafted.

**Phase 2 Success:** 1,000 monthly organic visitors; newsletter capturing subscribers; blog published on cadence.

---

## Phase 3 — Expansion

**Goal:** Deepen engagement and introduce first monetization signals.

**Rationale:** By Phase 3, the platform has an audience and established credibility. This is the right time to introduce tools and resources that demonstrate systematic expertise and begin positioning for productized offerings.

**Dependencies:** Phase 2 complete; audience size sufficient to justify resource investment (>500 monthly visitors).

---

## Phase 4 — Monetization

**Goal:** Generate revenue from the platform beyond consulting engagements.

**Rationale:** Consulting income is time-capped. Phase 4 introduces leverage — products and content that generate income independent of hourly availability.

**Dependencies:** Phase 3 complete; email list >500; established content cadence.

---

## Roadmap Governance

The roadmap is reviewed quarterly. Items are promoted, deprioritized, or removed based on:
- User behaviour data (analytics)
- Inbound lead conversion rate
- Business priorities
- Engineering effort vs. business value ratio
