## Product Summary

`portfolio-v1` is a premium personal brand platform for a Senior Data Engineering Consultant. It is not a résumé website. It is a long-term business asset that generates consulting leads, builds credibility, and supports future expansion into content, education, and productized services.

---

## Business Goals

1. Generate 3–5 qualified inbound consulting leads per month by month 3
2. Achieve 1,000+ monthly unique visitors by month 6 via organic search
3. Publish 2 technical articles per month to compound SEO value
4. Close at least 1 consulting engagement traceable to the platform within 6 months
5. Build an email subscriber base of 200+ within the first year

---

| Priority | Segment | Primary Goal |
